Measures of Location & Spread

10 exam-style questions on mean, median, mode, variance, standard deviation and quartiles. Tap Show answer for full working.

Q1Easy3 marks

Find the mean, median and mode of the data set: 3, 5, 5, 7, 8, 9, 9, 9, 12.

Mean: \( \dfrac{67}{9}\approx7.44 \). Median: 8. Mode: 9.

Q2Easy4 marks

The mean of 5 numbers is 12. Four of the numbers are 8, 10, 15 and 11. Find the fifth number.

Sum \( =5\times12=60 \). Fifth \( =60-(8+10+15+11)=60-44=16 \).

Q3Easy4 marks

Calculate the range and interquartile range for: 2, 4, 5, 7, 9, 11, 12, 15, 18.

Range: \( 18-2=16 \). \( Q_1=5 \), \( Q_3=12 \), IQR \( =7 \).

Q4Medium5 marks

The data 4, 6, 7, 9, 10, 12 have mean \( \bar{x} \). Find \( \sum(x-\bar{x})^2 \) and hence the variance and standard deviation (population formulae).

\( \bar{x}=8 \). Deviations: \( -4,-2,-1,1,2,4 \). Squared sum \( =16+4+1+1+4+16=42 \). Variance \( =\dfrac{42}{6}=7 \). SD \( =\sqrt{7} \).

Q5Medium5 marks

A data set has \( n=20 \), \( \sum x=240 \), \( \sum x^2=3200 \). Find the mean and the standard deviation.

Mean \( =\dfrac{240}{20}=12 \). Variance \( =\dfrac{3200}{20}-12^2=160-144=16 \). SD \( =4 \).

Q6Medium6 marks

The heights (cm) of 8 students are: 162, 158, 170, 165, 172, 160, 168, 175. Find the mean and sample standard deviation (divisor \( n-1 \)).

Mean \( =\dfrac{1330}{8}=166.25 \). \( \sum(x-\bar{x})^2\approx246.5 \). Sample variance \( \approx\dfrac{246.5}{7}\approx35.21 \). Sample SD \( \approx5.93 \) cm.

Q7Medium6 marks

A frequency distribution for discrete data: \( x=1,2,3,4,5 \) with frequencies 4, 6, 8, 5, 2. Find the mean and the mode.

\( \sum f=25 \), \( \sum fx=1\cdot4+2\cdot6+3\cdot8+4\cdot5+5\cdot2=4+12+24+20+10=70 \). Mean \( =\dfrac{70}{25}=2.8 \). Mode \( =3 \).

Q8Hard7 marks

For a set of data, \( \sum x=150 \), \( \sum x^2=2500 \), \( n=10 \). A new observation of 20 is added. Find the new mean and the new standard deviation (population).

Old mean \( =15 \). New mean \( =\dfrac{170}{11}\approx15.455 \). New \( \sum x^2=2500+400=2900 \). Variance \( =\dfrac{2900}{11}-\left(\dfrac{170}{11}\right)^2\approx263.636-238.843\approx24.793 \). SD \( \approx4.98 \).

Q9Hard7 marks

The coded data \( y=\dfrac{x-50}{10} \) has mean \( \bar{y}=1.2 \) and SD \( s_y=0.8 \). Find the mean and standard deviation of \( x \).

\( \bar{x}=10\bar{y}+50=12+50=62 \). \( s_x=10s_y=8 \).

Q10Hard8 marks

A data set of 12 observations has mean 25 and variance 16. Two additional observations, both equal to 25, are added. Show that the mean is unchanged and find the new variance.

New mean still 25. Old \( \sum(x-\bar{x})^2=12\times16=192 \). New sum of squares of deviations still 192 (extra terms 0). New variance \( =\dfrac{192}{14}\approx13.71 \).
