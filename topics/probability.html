Q1Easy3 marks

A fair six-sided die is rolled once. Find the probability that the score is (a) even, (b) greater than 4, (c) a multiple of 3.

(a) \( \dfrac{3}{6} = \dfrac{1}{2} \). (b) \( \dfrac{2}{6} = \dfrac{1}{3} \). (c) \( \dfrac{2}{6} = \dfrac{1}{3} \).

Q2Easy4 marks

Two fair coins are tossed. Find the probability of (a) two heads, (b) at least one head, (c) exactly one head.

Sample space: HH, HT, TH, TT. (a) \( \dfrac{1}{4} \). (b) \( \dfrac{3}{4} \). (c) \( \dfrac{2}{4} = \dfrac{1}{2} \).

Q3Easy4 marks

Events \( A \) and \( B \) are independent with \( P(A)=0.4 \) and \( P(B)=0.3 \). Find \( P(A \cap B) \) and \( P(A \cup B) \).

\( P(A \cap B)=0.4 \times 0.3=0.12 \). \( P(A \cup B)=0.4+0.3-0.12=0.58 \).

Q4Medium5 marks

A bag contains 5 red and 3 blue discs. Two discs are drawn at random without replacement. Find the probability that (a) both are red, (b) they are different colours.

(a) \( \dfrac{5}{8} \times \dfrac{4}{7} = \dfrac{20}{56} = \dfrac{5}{14} \). (b) \( \dfrac{5}{8}\times\dfrac{3}{7} + \dfrac{3}{8}\times\dfrac{5}{7} = \dfrac{15}{56}+\dfrac{15}{56}=\dfrac{30}{56}=\dfrac{15}{28} \).

Q5Medium6 marks

In a group of 40 students, 25 study Maths, 18 study Physics and 10 study both. A student is chosen at random. Find the probability that the student studies (a) Maths or Physics, (b) neither subject.

\( |M \cup P| = 25+18-10=33 \). (a) \( \dfrac{33}{40} \). (b) \( \dfrac{7}{40} \).

Q6Medium6 marks

The probability that it rains on a given day is 0.3. If it rains, the probability that a bus is late is 0.4; if it does not rain, the probability the bus is late is 0.1. Find the probability that (a) the bus is late, (b) it rained given that the bus is late.

(a) \( P(L)=0.3\times0.4 + 0.7\times0.1 = 0.12+0.07=0.19 \). (b) \( P(R|L)=\dfrac{0.12}{0.19}=\dfrac{12}{19} \).

Q7Medium6 marks

Events \( A \) and \( B \) satisfy \( P(A)=0.5 \), \( P(B)=0.6 \) and \( P(A \cup B)=0.8 \). Find \( P(A \cap B) \) and determine whether \( A \) and \( B \) are independent.

\( P(A \cap B)=0.5+0.6-0.8=0.3 \). \( P(A)P(B)=0.3 \), so yes, independent (since product equals intersection).

Q8Hard7 marks

A factory has two machines. Machine A produces 60% of items and Machine B 40%. The probability an item from A is defective is 0.02; from B it is 0.05. An item is chosen at random and found to be defective. Find the probability it came from Machine B.

\( P(D)=0.6\times0.02 + 0.4\times0.05 = 0.012+0.02=0.032 \). \( P(B|D)=\dfrac{0.4\times0.05}{0.032}=\dfrac{0.02}{0.032}=\dfrac{5}{8} \).

Q9Hard7 marks

Three cards are drawn at random without replacement from a standard pack of 52 cards. Find the probability that all three are hearts, and the probability that exactly two are hearts.

All hearts: \( \dfrac{13}{52}\times\dfrac{12}{51}\times\dfrac{11}{50}=\dfrac{1716}{132600}=\dfrac{11}{850} \). Exactly two: \( \dbinom{3}{2}\times\dfrac{13}{52}\times\dfrac{12}{51}\times\dfrac{39}{50}=\dfrac{3\times13\times12\times39}{52\times51\times50}=\dfrac{3\times13\times12\times39}{132600}=\dfrac{117}{850} \) (after simplifying).

Q10Hard8 marks

The events \( A \) and \( B \) are such that \( P(A)=0.4 \), \( P(B|A)=0.5 \) and \( P(B|A')=0.3 \). Find \( P(B) \) and \( P(A|B) \).

\( P(B)=P(B|A)P(A)+P(B|A')P(A')=0.5\times0.4 + 0.3\times0.6=0.2+0.18=0.38 \). \( P(A|B)=\dfrac{P(B|A)P(A)}{P(B)}=\dfrac{0.2}{0.38}=\dfrac{10}{19} \).
