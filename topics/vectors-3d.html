Vectors (3D)

12 exam-style questions on 3D vectors: \(\mathbf{i},\mathbf{j},\mathbf{k}\) notation, magnitude, distance, position vectors, unit vectors and simple geometric problems. Tap Show answer for full working.

Q1 Easy 4 marks

Find the magnitude of the vector \(\mathbf{a}=2\mathbf{i}-3\mathbf{j}+6\mathbf{k}\).

\(|\mathbf{a}|=\sqrt{2^2+(-3)^2+6^2}=\sqrt{4+9+36}=\sqrt{49}=7\).

Q2 Easy 4 marks

The points \(A\) and \(B\) have coordinates \((1,2,3)\) and \((4,-1,5)\) respectively. Find the vector \(\overrightarrow{AB}\) and the distance \(AB\).

\(\overrightarrow{AB}=(4-1)\mathbf{i}+(-1-2)\mathbf{j}+(5-3)\mathbf{k}=3\mathbf{i}-3\mathbf{j}+2\mathbf{k}\).

\(AB=\sqrt{9+9+4}=\sqrt{22}\).

Q3 Easy 4 marks

Find a unit vector in the direction of \(\mathbf{v}=4\mathbf{i}-4\mathbf{j}+7\mathbf{k}\).

\(|\mathbf{v}|=\sqrt{16+16+49}=\sqrt{81}=9\).

Unit vector \(=\dfrac{1}{9}(4\mathbf{i}-4\mathbf{j}+7\mathbf{k})=\dfrac{4}{9}\mathbf{i}-\dfrac{4}{9}\mathbf{j}+\dfrac{7}{9}\mathbf{k}\).

Q4 Easy 5 marks

Given \(\mathbf{a}=3\mathbf{i}+\mathbf{j}-2\mathbf{k}\) and \(\mathbf{b}=\mathbf{i}-4\mathbf{j}+\mathbf{k}\), find \(2\mathbf{a}-3\mathbf{b}\).

\(2\mathbf{a}=6\mathbf{i}+2\mathbf{j}-4\mathbf{k}\), \(3\mathbf{b}=3\mathbf{i}-12\mathbf{j}+3\mathbf{k}\).

\(2\mathbf{a}-3\mathbf{b}=(6-3)\mathbf{i}+(2-(-12))\mathbf{j}+(-4-3)\mathbf{k}=3\mathbf{i}+14\mathbf{j}-7\mathbf{k}\).

Q5 Medium 6 marks

The points \(A\), \(B\) and \(C\) have position vectors \(\mathbf{a}=2\mathbf{i}+\mathbf{j}+3\mathbf{k}\), \(\mathbf{b}=5\mathbf{i}-\mathbf{j}+\mathbf{k}\) and \(\mathbf{c}=8\mathbf{i}-3\mathbf{j}-\mathbf{k}\) relative to a fixed origin \(O\).

Show that \(A\), \(B\) and \(C\) are collinear.

\(\overrightarrow{AB}=\mathbf{b}-\mathbf{a}=3\mathbf{i}-2\mathbf{j}-2\mathbf{k}\).

\(\overrightarrow{AC}=\mathbf{c}-\mathbf{a}=6\mathbf{i}-4\mathbf{j}-4\mathbf{k}=2(3\mathbf{i}-2\mathbf{j}-2\mathbf{k})=2\overrightarrow{AB}\).

Since \(\overrightarrow{AC}\) is a scalar multiple of \(\overrightarrow{AB}\), the points are collinear.

Q6 Medium 6 marks

Find the value of \(k\) such that the vectors \(\mathbf{a}=2\mathbf{i}-3\mathbf{j}+\mathbf{k}\) and \(\mathbf{b}=4\mathbf{i}-6\mathbf{j}+k\mathbf{k}\) are parallel.

For parallel vectors, \(\mathbf{b}=\lambda\mathbf{a}\) for some scalar \(\lambda\).

\(4=2\lambda\Rightarrow\lambda=2\), \(-6=-3\lambda\Rightarrow\lambda=2\), and \(k=\lambda\cdot 1=2\).

Hence \(k=2\).

Q7 Medium 6 marks

The position vectors of points \(A\) and \(B\) relative to an origin \(O\) are \(\mathbf{a}=4\mathbf{i}+\mathbf{j}-2\mathbf{k}\) and \(\mathbf{b}=2\mathbf{i}-3\mathbf{j}+\mathbf{k}\). Find the position vector of the midpoint of \(AB\) and the distance of this midpoint from \(O\).

Midpoint \(M\) has position vector \(\mathbf{m}=\dfrac{1}{2}(\mathbf{a}+\mathbf{b})=\dfrac{1}{2}(6\mathbf{i}-2\mathbf{j}-\mathbf{k})=3\mathbf{i}-\mathbf{j}-\dfrac{1}{2}\mathbf{k}\).

\(OM=\sqrt{9+1+\dfrac{1}{4}}=\sqrt{\dfrac{41}{4}}=\dfrac{\sqrt{41}}{2}\).

Q8 Medium 7 marks

Given \(\mathbf{p}=\mathbf{i}+2\mathbf{j}-2\mathbf{k}\) and \(\mathbf{q}=2\mathbf{i}-\mathbf{j}+2\mathbf{k}\), find the angle between \(\mathbf{p}\) and \(\mathbf{q}\).

\(\mathbf{p}\cdot\mathbf{q}=1\cdot2+2\cdot(-1)+(-2)\cdot2=2-2-4=-4\).

\(|\mathbf{p}|=\sqrt{1+4+4}=3\), \(|\mathbf{q}|=\sqrt{4+1+4}=3\).

\(\cos\theta=\dfrac{-4}{3\cdot3}=-\dfrac{4}{9}\). \(\theta=\cos^{-1}\bigl(-\dfrac{4}{9}\bigr)\approx116.4^\circ\).

Q9 Hard 7 marks

The points \(A\), \(B\) and \(C\) have position vectors \(2\mathbf{i}+\mathbf{j}-\mathbf{k}\), \(5\mathbf{i}-2\mathbf{j}+\mathbf{k}\) and \(4\mathbf{i}+3\mathbf{j}-\mathbf{k}\) respectively.

(a) Find \(\overrightarrow{AB}\) and \(\overrightarrow{AC}\).

(b) Show that \(\overrightarrow{AB}\) is perpendicular to \(\overrightarrow{AC}\).

(a) \(\overrightarrow{AB}=(5-2)\mathbf{i}+(-2-1)\mathbf{j}+(1-(-1))\mathbf{k}=3\mathbf{i}-3\mathbf{j}+2\mathbf{k}\).

\(\overrightarrow{AC}=(4-2)\mathbf{i}+(3-1)\mathbf{j}+(-1-(-1))\mathbf{k}=2\mathbf{i}+2\mathbf{j}+0\mathbf{k}\).

(b) \(\overrightarrow{AB}\cdot\overrightarrow{AC}=3\cdot2+(-3)\cdot2+2\cdot0=6-6+0=0\). Hence \(\overrightarrow{AB}\) is perpendicular to \(\overrightarrow{AC}\).

Q10 Hard 8 marks

Relative to a fixed origin \(O\), the position vectors of points \(A\) and \(B\) are \(3\mathbf{i}-2\mathbf{j}+\mathbf{k}\) and \(\mathbf{i}+4\mathbf{j}-2\mathbf{k}\) respectively. The point \(C\) divides the line segment \(AB\) in the ratio \(2:1\).

(a) Find the position vector of \(C\).

(b) Find the distance \(OC\).

(a) \(C\) divides \(AB\) in the ratio \(2:1\), so \(\mathbf{c}=\dfrac{1\cdot\mathbf{a}+2\cdot\mathbf{b}}{3}=\dfrac{(3\mathbf{i}-2\mathbf{j}+\mathbf{k})+2(\mathbf{i}+4\mathbf{j}-2\mathbf{k})}{3}=\dfrac{5\mathbf{i}+6\mathbf{j}-3\mathbf{k}}{3}=\dfrac{5}{3}\mathbf{i}+2\mathbf{j}-\mathbf{k}\).

(b) \(OC=\sqrt{\bigl(\dfrac{5}{3}\bigr)^2+4+1}=\sqrt{\dfrac{25}{9}+5}=\sqrt{\dfrac{25+45}{9}}=\sqrt{\dfrac{70}{9}}=\dfrac{\sqrt{70}}{3}\).

Q11 Hard 7 marks

The vector \(\mathbf{a}=2\mathbf{i}-\mathbf{j}+2\mathbf{k}\) makes an angle \(\theta\) with the positive \(z\)-axis. Find the exact value of \(\cos\theta\).

The unit vector in the direction of the positive \(z\)-axis is \(\mathbf{k}\).

\(\cos\theta=\dfrac{\mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{k}}{|\mathbf{a}|\,|\mathbf{k}|}=\dfrac{2}{\sqrt{4+1+4}\cdot1}=\dfrac{2}{3}\).

Q12 Hard 8 marks

The points \(A\), \(B\) and \(C\) have position vectors \(\mathbf{a}=\mathbf{i}+2\mathbf{j}+3\mathbf{k}\), \(\mathbf{b}=4\mathbf{i}+\mathbf{j}-\mathbf{k}\) and \(\mathbf{c}=10\mathbf{i}-\mathbf{j}-9\mathbf{k}\).

(a) Find \(\overrightarrow{AB}\) and \(\overrightarrow{BC}\).

(b) Show that \(A\), \(B\) and \(C\) are collinear.

(c) Find the ratio in which \(B\) divides the line segment \(AC\).

(a) \(\overrightarrow{AB}=(4-1)\mathbf{i}+(1-2)\mathbf{j}+(-1-3)\mathbf{k}=3\mathbf{i}-\mathbf{j}-4\mathbf{k}\).

\(\overrightarrow{BC}=(10-4)\mathbf{i}+(-1-1)\mathbf{j}+(-9-(-1))\mathbf{k}=6\mathbf{i}-2\mathbf{j}-8\mathbf{k}\).

(b) \(\overrightarrow{BC}=6\mathbf{i}-2\mathbf{j}-8\mathbf{k}=2(3\mathbf{i}-\mathbf{j}-4\mathbf{k})=2\overrightarrow{AB}\). Since \(\overrightarrow{BC}\) is a scalar multiple of \(\overrightarrow{AB}\), the points are collinear.

(c) \(\overrightarrow{AB}:\overrightarrow{BC}=1:2\), so \(B\) divides \(AC\) in the ratio \(1:2\).
