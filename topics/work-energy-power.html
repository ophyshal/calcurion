Work, Energy & Power

12 exam-style questions on work done by forces, kinetic and potential energy, conservation of energy and power. Tap Show answer for full working.

4 Easy 4 Medium 4 Hard
Q1 Easy 4 marks

A constant force of \(20\,\mathrm{N}\) acts on a particle, moving it a distance of \(5\,\mathrm{m}\) in the direction of the force. Find the work done by the force.

Work \(=Fd=20\cdot5=100\,\mathrm{J}\).

Q2 Easy 5 marks

A particle of mass \(3\,\mathrm{kg}\) is moving with speed \(4\,\mathrm{m\,s}^{-1}\). Find its kinetic energy. A resistive force does \(18\,\mathrm{J}\) of work against the motion. Find the new speed of the particle.

KE \(=\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot3\cdot16=24\,\mathrm{J}\). New KE \(=24-18=6\,\mathrm{J}\). \(\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot3\cdot v^2=6\Rightarrow v^2=4\Rightarrow v=2\,\mathrm{m\,s}^{-1}\).

Q3 Easy 4 marks

A particle of mass \(2\,\mathrm{kg}\) is raised vertically through a height of \(5\,\mathrm{m}\). Taking \(g=10\,\mathrm{m\,s}^{-2}\), find the gain in potential energy.

PE gain \(=mgh=2\cdot10\cdot5=100\,\mathrm{J}\).

Q4 Easy 5 marks

A car of mass \(1000\,\mathrm{kg}\) travels at a constant speed of \(20\,\mathrm{m\,s}^{-1}\) against a constant resistance of \(400\,\mathrm{N}\). Find the power developed by the engine.

At constant speed, driving force \(=\) resistance \(=400\,\mathrm{N}\). Power \(=Fv=400\cdot20=8000\,\mathrm{W}=8\,\mathrm{kW}\).

Q5 Medium 6 marks

A particle of mass \(4\,\mathrm{kg}\) slides down a smooth plane inclined at \(30^\circ\) to the horizontal, starting from rest. Find its speed after it has travelled \(10\,\mathrm{m}\) down the plane. Take \(g=10\,\mathrm{m\,s}^{-2}\).

Loss in PE \(=mgh=4\cdot10\cdot10\sin30^\circ=200\,\mathrm{J}\). Gain in KE \(=\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot4\cdot v^2=2v^2\). \(2v^2=200\Rightarrow v^2=100\Rightarrow v=10\,\mathrm{m\,s}^{-1}\).

Q6 Medium 7 marks

A particle of mass \(m\) is projected up a rough plane inclined at angle \(\alpha\) with speed \(u\). The coefficient of friction is \(\mu\). Use energy methods to find the distance travelled up the plane before the particle comes to instantaneous rest.

Work done against friction \(=\mu mg\cos\alpha\cdot s\). Loss in KE \(=\dfrac{1}{2}mu^2\). Gain in PE \(=mg\sin\alpha\cdot s\).

\(\dfrac{1}{2}mu^2=mg\sin\alpha\cdot s+\mu mg\cos\alpha\cdot s\Rightarrow s=\dfrac{u^2}{2g(\sin\alpha+\mu\cos\alpha)}\).

Q7 Medium 6 marks

A force \(\mathbf{F}=(3\mathbf{i}+4\mathbf{j})\,\mathrm{N}\) moves a particle from the point with position vector \(\mathbf{i}+2\mathbf{j}\) to the point with position vector \(5\mathbf{i}+8\mathbf{j}\). Find the work done by the force.

Displacement \(\mathbf{d}=(5-1)\mathbf{i}+(8-2)\mathbf{j}=4\mathbf{i}+6\mathbf{j}\). Work \(=\mathbf{F}\cdot\mathbf{d}=12+24=36\,\mathrm{J}\).

Q8 Medium 6 marks

A car of mass \(1200\,\mathrm{kg}\) accelerates from \(10\,\mathrm{m\,s}^{-1}\) to \(25\,\mathrm{m\,s}^{-1}\) over a distance of \(200\,\mathrm{m}\) against a constant resistance of \(300\,\mathrm{N}\). Find the work done by the driving force and the average power developed if the time taken is \(12\,\mathrm{s}\).

Change in KE \(=\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot1200(625-100)=300\cdot525=157500\,\mathrm{J}\). Work against resistance \(=300\cdot200=60000\,\mathrm{J}\). Work by driving force \(=217500\,\mathrm{J}\).

Average power \(=\dfrac{217500}{12}=18125\,\mathrm{W}\).

Q9 Hard 8 marks

A particle of mass \(2\,\mathrm{kg}\) moves on a smooth horizontal surface under the action of a force \(\mathbf{F}=(4x\mathbf{i}-2y\mathbf{j})\,\mathrm{N}\), where the position is \(\mathbf{r}=x\mathbf{i}+y\mathbf{j}\) metres. The particle starts at the origin with speed \(3\,\mathrm{m\,s}^{-1}\) in the direction of the positive \(x\)-axis. Find the speed of the particle when it first reaches the point \((2,0)\).

Work done \(=\int\mathbf{F}\cdot\mathrm{d}\mathbf{r}\). Along the \(x\)-axis \(y=0\), \(\mathrm{d}y=0\), so work \(=\int_0^2 4x\,\mathrm{d}x=2x^2\big|_0^2=8\,\mathrm{J}\).

Initial KE \(=\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot2\cdot9=9\,\mathrm{J}\). Final KE \(=9+8=17\,\mathrm{J}\). \(\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot2\cdot v^2=17\Rightarrow v=\sqrt{17}\,\mathrm{m\,s}^{-1}\).

Q10 Hard 7 marks

A particle slides down a rough plane inclined at \(30^\circ\) to the horizontal. The coefficient of friction is \(0.2\). The particle starts from rest. Use energy methods to find the speed after it has travelled \(8\,\mathrm{m}\) down the plane. Take \(g=10\,\mathrm{m\,s}^{-2}\).

Loss in PE \(=mg\cdot8\sin30^\circ=mg\cdot4\). Work against friction \(=\mu mg\cos30^\circ\cdot8=0.2\cdot mg\cdot\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\cdot8=0.8\sqrt{3}\,mg\).

KE gain \(=4mg-0.8\sqrt{3}\,mg=mg(4-0.8\sqrt{3})\). \(v^2=2g(4-0.8\sqrt{3})\approx2\cdot10\cdot(4-1.386)=52.28\). \(v\approx7.23\,\mathrm{m\,s}^{-1}\).

Q11 Hard 8 marks

A pump raises water from a reservoir and delivers it at a rate of \(0.2\,\mathrm{m}^3\,\mathrm{s}^{-1}\) with speed \(12\,\mathrm{m\,s}^{-1}\) at a height of \(25\,\mathrm{m}\) above the reservoir. Taking the density of water as \(1000\,\mathrm{kg\,m}^{-3}\) and \(g=10\,\mathrm{m\,s}^{-2}\), find the power output of the pump, neglecting energy losses.

Mass per second \(=0.2\cdot1000=200\,\mathrm{kg\,s}^{-1}\).

Power for PE \(=200\cdot10\cdot25=50000\,\mathrm{W}\). Power for KE \(=\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot200\cdot144=14400\,\mathrm{W}\). Total power \(=64400\,\mathrm{W}=64.4\,\mathrm{kW}\).

Q12 Hard 7 marks

A particle of mass \(m\) moves under a force \(\mathbf{F}=-kr\mathbf{\hat{r}}\) (directed towards the origin), where \(r=|\mathbf{r}|\). Show that the total mechanical energy is conserved, and find the work done by the force in moving the particle from \(r=a\) to \(r=b\).

The force is conservative (central and magnitude depends only on \(r\)). Potential \(V=\dfrac{1}{2}kr^2\) (up to a constant). Total energy \(\dfrac{1}{2}m\dot{r}^2+\dfrac{1}{2}kr^2\) is constant.

Work from \(a\) to \(b\) \(=-\Delta V=-\dfrac{1}{2}k(b^2-a^2)\).
